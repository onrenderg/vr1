Privacy Policy for Arcade Cockpit

Effective Date: July 29, 2026

At Arcade Cockpit, accessible from https://onrenderg.github.io/vr1/, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Arcade Cockpit and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Arcade Cockpit operates as a WebXR/PWA application for Meta Horizon OS. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), telemetry, or user tracking data to remote servers.

Virtual Reality & Sensors

Arcade Cockpit utilizes WebXR APIs strictly for rendering VR graphics and processing local controller inputs inside your headset. All VR tracking and sensor data remain processing locally on your device and are never recorded or transmitted.

Local Storage & Cookies

Arcade Cockpit uses local browser storage exclusively for saving your game preferences, high scores, and offline PWA assets. No tracking cookies or third-party analytical trackers are used.

Children's Information

Arcade Cockpit does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided such information on our website, we encourage you to contact us immediately.

Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at apkntech@gmail.com.
